Who We Are

Redefining What a Crown Means

Miss Miami was founded on the belief that beauty pageants can be more than surface-level competition. We created a space where accomplished, driven women come together to celebrate what makes them extraordinary.

Our contestants are students, entrepreneurs, artists, athletes and advocates. They carry causes close to their hearts and the ambition to make real change in their communities.

Every year, Miss Miami brings together the brightest women of South Florida for an experience that challenges, transforms and elevates — whether you win the title or not, you leave as part of a lifelong sisterhood.

What We Stand For

Beauty. Confidence. Purpose.

Three words that define every Miss Miami contestant — and every woman who walks through our doors.

✦

Beauty

We celebrate beauty in all its forms — inner radiance, authentic confidence, and the elegance of a woman who knows her worth. Beauty is not a standard. It is a presence.

♛

Confidence

The ability to walk into a room and own it. To speak with conviction and grace under pressure. We cultivate confidence that goes far beyond the stage — and lasts a lifetime.

◈

Purpose

Every Miss Miami contestant brings a point of view and a cause that drives her. We believe that with influence comes responsibility, and we give each woman space to create meaningful impact.
