Privacy Policy

Last updated September 2, 2026

This policy explains what information Miss Miami collects, why we use it and the choices available to you when you use our website, submit an application or contact our team.

1. Information We Collect

We collect information that you choose to provide through the Miss Miami website.

Contestant applications

  • contact details, date of birth, residential information and eligibility responses;
  • education, occupation, background, social profiles and information about your interests, experience and goals;
  • application photographs and an introduction video;
  • payment and transaction details provided through our payment processor. Miss Miami does not intend to store complete card numbers;
  • consents, acknowledgements and application status information.

Approved contestants

After approval, a contestant uploads only one government-issued photo ID for identity and eligibility verification. No additional identity documents are requested.

Other website visitors

We may collect contact information and message content submitted through the Contact or Sponsor forms, together with basic technical information such as browser type, device type, approximate location derived from an IP address and website activity.

2. How We Use Information

  • to receive, review and manage contestant applications;
  • to process the registration fee and maintain transaction records;
  • to confirm identity and eligibility after approval;
  • to create and operate contestant and organizer accounts;
  • to manage application statuses and the Road to the Crown program;
  • to respond to questions and partnership inquiries;
  • to send service messages, application decisions and program updates;
  • to protect the website, investigate misuse and comply with legal obligations;
  • to improve the website and understand how it is used.

3. Photographs, Video and Publicity

Application media is used to evaluate applications and administer the competition. If you are selected, photographs, video and approved profile information may also be used for Miss Miami publicity, website content, social media, event coverage and other promotional purposes as described in the application consents and official rules.

4. How We Share Information

We may share information with trusted providers that help us operate the platform, including hosting, database, file storage, payment, email and analytics providers. We may also share necessary information with authorized Miss Miami team members, event professionals and advisors who need it to perform their work.

We may disclose information when required by law, to protect safety or rights, or in connection with a legitimate organizational transfer. We do not sell personal information.

5. Cookies and Analytics

The website may use essential browser storage and, when enabled, analytics tools to understand traffic and performance. Where required, visitors will be given an appropriate choice regarding non-essential cookies or similar technologies.

6. Retention and Security

We keep information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this policy, to maintain appropriate records and to meet legal obligations. Identity documents should be retained only for the period needed to complete verification and resolve related issues.

We use reasonable administrative, technical and organizational safeguards. No online service can guarantee absolute security, so please do not send card details or sensitive identity information through the general Contact or Sponsor forms.

7. Your Choices

You may ask to access, correct or delete personal information, subject to applicable law and legitimate recordkeeping requirements. You may also unsubscribe from optional marketing messages while continuing to receive necessary application or account communications.

8. Age and Eligibility

Miss Miami applications are intended for people who meet the eligibility requirements stated in the official rules. The contestant application is not directed to children.

9. Third-Party Links

Our website may link to third-party websites and services. Their privacy practices are governed by their own policies.

10. Updates to This Policy

We may update this policy as the platform and applicable requirements develop. The latest version will be posted on this page with a revised update date.

11. Contact Us

For privacy questions or requests, email info@missmiamiofficial.com.
