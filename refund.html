Miss Miami 2027

Refund Policy

Last updated September 2, 2026

This policy applies to the Miss Miami contestant application and registration fee. Separate written terms may apply to sponsorships or other future purchases.

1. Application Fee

The Miss Miami application and registration fee is $50. The fee covers application processing and related administrative work.

2. General Refund Rule

The application fee is non-refundable once the paid application has been submitted. This includes applications that are not approved, applicants who withdraw and contestants who later choose not to participate.

3. Duplicate or Incorrect Charges

If the same application is charged more than once because of a verified technical or payment-processing error, please contact us. After confirming the duplicate or incorrect charge, we will arrange the appropriate correction or refund.

4. Failed or Incomplete Payments

A failed or incomplete payment does not create a completed application. If a pending charge is not completed, the timing of its release is controlled by the bank or payment provider.

5. Event or Program Changes

The application fee is paid for application review and registration processing, not for admission to a specific event, prize or title. Changes to preparation schedules, venues, partners or program details therefore do not normally make the application fee refundable.

If Miss Miami is unable to process an application for reasons within its control, the team will review the circumstances and provide any refund required by applicable law.

6. How to Report a Payment Problem

Email info@missmiamiofficial.com with the applicant’s name, application email, payment date, amount and a short description of the issue. Do not send complete card details by email.

7. Processing Time

Approved refunds are returned to the original payment method. The time required for the credit to appear depends on the payment provider and the cardholder’s financial institution.

8. Legal Rights

Nothing in this policy limits a right or remedy that cannot be waived under applicable law.
