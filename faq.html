Application & Selection

Visit the Apply page, review the current rules and complete every required section of the application form. Submission of an application does not guarantee acceptance into the competition.

The current form requests one recent, clear and unfiltered headshot, one recent, clear and unfiltered full-length photo, and a one-minute introduction video about you and your goals.

The confirmed application deadline will be published on the official Miss Miami website. Applicants should review the Apply page for the latest approved information before submitting.

Completed applications are reviewed by the Miss Miami team. The decision is communicated using the email address provided in the application. Review timing and any additional selection steps will be shared through official communication.

If you notice an error after submission, use the Contact page and identify the information that needs correction. The team will advise whether an update can be made at that stage of review.

The Contestant Portal is the private space intended for approved contestants. If an application is approved, the decision email will include instructions for activating access with the email address used in the application.

Use the Contact page and describe the issue, the device you are using and the step where the problem occurred. Do not send payment-card details through a general contact message.
